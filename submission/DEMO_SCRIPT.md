# BillBack: 1-minute demo video script

Length: 0:56. Video file: `submission/bill-back-demo.mp4`. Narration is an AI voice (Gemini TTS). All documents shown are fictional.

| # | Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00-0:07 | Title slide | BillBack finds potential errors in a medical bill, and drafts the dispute letter, in about a minute. |
| 2 | 0:07-0:43 | Live site: upload fictional ER bill, $620 found, issue cards, read-back table, Draft my letter, share card | Start by uploading a photo of a bill. This one is fictional. Gemini reads every line item, and BillBack flags potential issues. Here, the same chest X-ray was billed twice, and the printed total is three hundred dollars higher than the line items. Each issue shows the amount, why it looks wrong, a confidence score, and what to ask for. You can also check exactly what was read from your document. Click Draft my letter, and Gemini writes a specific, polite dispute letter you can copy or download. Finally, share a savings card that shows only the numbers, never your document. |
| 3 | 0:43-0:52 | Live site: upload fictional denial letter, $1,400 appealable, appeal to insurer drafted | It works for insurance denials too. Upload a denial letter, and BillBack flags it as appealable and drafts an appeal to the insurer. |
| 4 | 0:52-0:56 | Closing slide | BillBack. The patient's side of the billing department. |
